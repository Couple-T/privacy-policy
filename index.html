1. Data we process

When someone messages Feeed me through Messenger or Instagram, Meta may provide information needed to receive, respond to, and manage the conversation. We may process:

CategoryExamples
Meta account and message dataPlatform user ID, display name or username, profile information made available by Meta, message content, message ID, timestamps, and the channel used.
Content sent to usPhotos, attachments, and information voluntarily included in a message, such as pet details, delivery details, order information, or payment-slip images.
Support recordsConversation history, our replies, case notes, escalation records, and information needed to resolve a request.
Technical and security dataWebhook delivery data, request logs, and identifiers used to secure the Service, prevent duplicate messages, troubleshoot errors, and prevent abuse.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request. We do not ask for passwords, full payment-card numbers, or government-issued ID through the Service.

2. How we use data

We use personal data to provide customer support, answer product questions, process and follow up on orders or payments when requested, personalize a conversation using its prior context, improve the Service, protect its security, and meet legal obligations.

Some conversations may be processed by an AI-assisted support tool to draft or provide responses. A human may review a conversation when it is escalated, requires help, or is needed for quality and safety checks.

3. When we share data

We do not sell personal data. We share data only as needed to operate the Service, including with:

  • Meta: to receive messages and send replies through Messenger and Instagram.
  • Service providers: cloud hosting, database, backup, customer-support, security, and AI service providers that process data for us under instructions or contractual safeguards.
  • Authorities or other parties: where required by law, to protect rights, safety, or security, or in connection with a corporate transaction.

Meta's handling of information is governed by Meta's own policies. This Privacy Policy covers Feeed me's processing of data received through Meta, not Meta's processing.

4. Retention and security

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes above, including support continuity, dispute resolution, security, legal obligations, and backup recovery. Backups are retained for up to 30 days before scheduled deletion. We then delete or de-identify data unless a longer period is required or allowed by law.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls, encrypted connections, and restricted service credentials. No system is completely secure, so please use care when deciding what to send in a message.

5. Your choices and data deletion

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to or restrict certain processing where applicable law provides those rights.

To request deletion of data received from Meta, email gozilla.tiamo@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Include the Messenger or Instagram account used to contact us and enough detail for us to locate the conversation. We may ask for information to verify the request. We will delete eligible data and notify you when the request is completed, subject to data we must retain for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

You can also manage permissions for apps and connected experiences through your Facebook or Instagram settings.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to consent to data processing. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal obligations change. We will post the revised version here and update the "Last updated" date.
